Kode With Klossy creates learning experiences and opportunities for young women and gender expansive youth. 
Our programs ignite a spark that fuels our scholars’ agency to pursue their passions in a tech-driven world. 
Technology has incredible power to change and influence the way we live our lives and the way we work. 
This is only becoming more and more true as innovation is happening faster and on a wider scale than ever before.
Unfortunately, the culture of technology tends to encourage only some identities; and access to education and opportunities is not equitable. 
We are here to combat that. 
We want to make sure that young people everywhere have the opportunity to wield the power of technology and pursue bright futures in STEAM.
Our instructional teams are at the heart of our learning experience. 
Through training and ongoing support, these educators are armed with the tools they need to create a powerful experience for our scholars. 
Our alumni are the future leaders in technology. 
Kode With Klossy provides continued support on their coding journey, far beyond their time in our programs. 
Kode With Klossy programs are free. 
Kode With Klossy scholars are curious, community-minded, creative individuals who want to explore the possibilities of technology. 
We welcome all applicants who identity as young women and gender expansive, ages 13-18 -- regardless of CS experience.
